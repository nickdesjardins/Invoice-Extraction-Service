Long-invoice trial, 7 October 2026. Times are UTC.

A one-off script built an invoice with 20 line items and one with 40, and sent each to both
default models through the service's own engine (prompt, schema, parsing, validation). Two of
the three runs also sent the playground's two-line email invoice first. The script was not
kept; these are the lines it printed, as they were captured on screen, where each line was cut
at 260 characters (which is why the error messages end mid-word).

Each line: model, document, seconds until the call returned, the provider's finish reason,
token counts (prompt, completion, and how much of the completion was reasoning), then the
outcome. "ok items=20/20 total=ok" means every line item came back and the total was right.
"finish=length" means the answer was cut off at the output cap.

For a call that failed before any answer arrived (the rate-limited call in the third run), the
finish reason and the token counts on its line are left over from the call before it.
