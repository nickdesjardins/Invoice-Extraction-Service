Applied AI · structured extraction

Messy text in. Validated invoice out.

This web service takes the text of an email, a till receipt or a scanned page and asks a free language model to fill in a fixed invoice form. If it gets no answer that fits the form, it asks a second model.

FastAPI · Pydantic v2 · OpenRouter · strict JSON schema · temperature 0

How the two models were chosen

Measured on 7 October 2026, 17:54 to 19:02 UTC

Free models come and go, so this is a dated snapshot, not a live reading. Each of the four free models on OpenRouter that advertised structured output was sent 7 documents in each of 3 passes: six invoices and receipts, one of them an invoice for services, and one meeting email that must be refused. Every call went through this service's own prompt, schema and validation, with a time limit of 30 s.

  • No answer: the call ended without one. A rate limit, a timeout, a rejected format or cut-off output.
  • Fully correct: of the calls that answered, those with every checked field right, or the meeting email refused.
  • Order of the models: a model that rejects the format, or whose usual answer takes more than half of the time limit, is left out. Of the rest, fewest wrong answers first, then fewest calls with no answer. A fallback replaces a failed call, never a wrong answer.
